Mastering Educational Prompts

A quick primer on how educators can craft high-impact AI instructions

Principle 1

Specify Pedagogical Role

Don't just ask AI for a quiz. Assign a persona: "Act as an expert 8th-grade STEM teacher specializing in inquiry-based learning." This primes higher cognitive depth.

Principle 2

Provide Concrete Constraints

Define time limits, state standards (e.g., CCSS or NGSS), reading levels, and preferred modalities (e.g., Socratic dialogue, formative exit ticket).

Principle 3

Demand Structure & Rubrics

Instruct the AI to format with distinct headers, markdown tables, teacher scripts, anticipated misconceptions, and ready-to-print student response sheets.
